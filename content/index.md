PhD student at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, building machine-learning models for regulatory genomics.

I work on the data-centric side of sequence-to-function modeling: how to design the data that makes these models better, and how to make the models themselves faster and more reliable. I'm increasingly interested in bringing these methods to RNA- and DNA-based therapeutics.
