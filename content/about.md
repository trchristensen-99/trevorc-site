I was born and raised in Seattle. I studied molecular biology at Claremont McKenna College, where I engineered DNA polymerases to synthesize modified nucleic acids relevant to aptamers and nucleic acid-based therapeutics.

After college I spent two years as a postbaccalaureate research fellow at the National Cancer Institute in Rockville, Maryland, characterizing pancreatic-cancer GWAS variants with RNA-seq and gene-regulatory-network inference. That work is where I moved from the bench toward computation.

I am now a PhD student at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, in [Peter Koo's lab](https://koolab.cshl.edu/), working on machine learning for regulatory genomics. The [Research](./research) page covers what I work on now, along with my publications.

## Writing

## About this site

Built with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz), a static-site generator for digital gardens. Source on [GitHub](https://github.com/trchristensen-99/trevorc-site), hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

The gear icon at the top right of every page opens a settings panel: text zoom, page opacity, background art, metadata fields, and a few other things. Everything there is stored locally in your browser and never leaves it.

The pixel-art landscapes in the background come from a handful of itch.io artists and shift with the time of day and season. Full credits are on the [site art page](./site-art), where you can also browse the collection and pin any scene as your background. For a full-bleed view of just the art, see the [background showcase](./background).
